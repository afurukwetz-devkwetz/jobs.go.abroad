  Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a legitimate agency?

Yes. Global Job Connect is a fully licensed international recruitment agency (License NEA-2025-0192). We have successfully placed over 500 professionals across the UK, USA, Canada, Germany, and Australia since 2020.

Do I have to pay before being placed?

We charge a one-time non-refundable administrative processing fee to cover background checks, document verification, and file preparation. This ensures only serious candidates enter our pool. The primary placement fees are covered by your future employer.

How long does the process take?

While timelines vary based on your profession and destination country, our average processing time from application to interview stage is 4-6 weeks.

What happens if my application is rejected?

If you do not meet the initial criteria, our team will provide feedback on what you need to improve (e.g., language scores, missing certifications). You can reapply once you meet the requirements.
